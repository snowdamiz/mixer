## Ownership, failure and accounting review

1. The private sealed CacheMode hooks preserve Frozen first-free selection, slot+1 work, and no-op publish/release defaults. Swift scratch exclusively borrows the owner's free word alongside its records and slots. The temporary Partial begins at zero, initializes the allowed mask only after charged record initialization, and moves its word into the actual owner. No allocation or ticket is added.
2. Swift admission charges 3 before checking width, allowed bits and active cardinality; it charges 2 before selected-record validation and the later single-word mutation. The nonzero word and length <=64 establish a valid trailing-zero shift including bit 63. Full inventory takes the bounded record scan and rejects orphan records. Zero capacity returns exhaustion. The allowed-mask helper is total even for an invalid length.
3. checked_record remains the token authority: owner, live nonzero nonce equality and actual resident entry with positive pin count. Release validates the selected bit before control ledger removal. A failed check/removal returns the original token and leaves records/bitmap untouched. After the fallible control decrement, statistics, entry count, record reset and setting the bit are infallible under the existing owner invariants. Release adds no work, fuel or stop poll, preserving terminal drain.
4. Acquisition selects without mutating the word. It reserves control live-pin capacity, then performs lookup/decode/eviction/publication through the existing transactional path. A failure abandons that reservation. The record and bitmap publish adjacent to the existing infallible statistics and nonce commit, before returning the token. The checked next nonce prevents exhaustion from creating a reused identity. Fresh holes can reuse slot numbers while stale tokens retain the old nonce and fail authority checks.
5. The owner u64 and scratch reference are included by existing size_of owner/Partial/Scratch/generic envelopes. Three complete DensePinInventory values were added separately to construction, operation and cleanup. This conservative named-storage inventory covers helper references, word/scalar/iterator/result/error transfers; it is not a stack or RSS claim. Exact compiled layouts and combined geometry remain pending. Existing real geometry tests join all 86 ticket capacity and consuming zero cleanup.
6. Success selection costs 5 credits regardless of selected slot; the first-slot warm hit grows 8->12 and initialization 4928->4929. Full-chain warm hit grows 260->264. There is no measured speedup claim. Existing checked meter/work/cancellation behavior remains cumulative, and the new publication write is precharged.

Corruption boundary: cardinality plus the selected-record check is not a complete audit of every record. A balanced bitmap corruption outside the selected slot can remain latent while a valid selected slot is used; choosing the wrongly marked occupied slot then fails. This is acceptable for a private word mutated only under these exclusive hooks, not an authorization to restore arbitrary untrusted bitmap bytes or a claim of arbitrary memory-corruption recovery. Release likewise checks its selected bit and range, preserving drain rather than scanning unrelated records.
