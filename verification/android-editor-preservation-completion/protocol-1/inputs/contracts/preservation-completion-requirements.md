The frozen focus-history candidate is not accepted. Independent findings
`a0cf5e8bff8ffee66f277b5e83c76b2778db13bd8eac558ae3ef65cdc007f5fd`
reproduce an ordinary post-attachment known engine rejection that cannot restore
the old Binding, despite the original transaction and native snapshot remaining
current. A separate two-renderer internal invariant control shows foreign scope
end changing another renderer's count. The latter is not a public exploit claim.

Prepare a coordinated private lifetime protocol before implementation. A successful
attach return does not finish the transaction's restoration obligation. Preserve
the exact original captured transaction and journal/candidate field scope through
post-attach validation and a known commit rejection with successful engine abort.
Eligible rollback must restore the old compatible Binding and geometry while
preserving the same Editable, text, selection, composition, settled predecessor
and exact renderer ownership. No replacement/rollback scope or equivalent new
Mutation may be minted to substitute for that captured original authority.

Do not revive a truly stale transaction or continue after a new loss, invalid
snapshot, terminal uncertainty or foreign candidate. Preserve the distinction
between ordinary known rejection with eligible restoration and genuinely failed
restoration. First observed fatal/ordinary information, retained busy custody and
exact superclass/native suffix obligations remain binding.

Successful engine commit through finish must remain scalar/reference-only: no
native getter, main-thread platform query, layout, setter, JNI, event, callback,
allocation or owner resumption may be introduced into that interval. Propose
explicit precommit validation and scalar completion responsibilities for the
retained scope, including success, known rejection/abort/rollback, unknown commit
outcome, attach failure, terminal lifecycle and counter imbalance. Preserve the
existing shared native count and release only the matching ownership last, after
required work/reporting; no counter reset, new asynchronous retry or broad owner/
core/coordinator change is selected. Any necessary private signature change must
be coordinated with root and independent review before dependent implementation.

Every begin/end/completion endpoint must establish exact renderer, Input, Record,
View, original fence/transaction, candidate/journal and counted-scope provenance
before mutating a scope or shared count. End/cleanup after terminal state may use
its already-captured identity, never current success authority or another
renderer's nonzero count. Foreign or replayed calls must leave both owners' counts
and scope state unchanged. No exact positive case may borrow an unrelated count.

Keep both required failing witnesses and all previous ordinary, lifecycle,
rollback, repeated-loss, reentry, descriptor and SDK controls. A correction must
exercise the complete real coordinator rejection path, not only direct restore
inside attach. Independent actual-source review remains required. Canonical
integration, native qualification, public V2 and GA remain unselected.
